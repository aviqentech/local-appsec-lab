# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of another person's secrets or security evidence are unacceptable. Critique technical claims with evidence, accept corrections and keep disagreements focused on the work.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or ban repeat offenders, with a concise explanation where safe. For a private conduct report, request a confidential contact from the maintainers without posting identifying details in public. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities rather than conduct concerns.

Maintainers should protect reporter privacy, avoid retaliation and consider context before choosing proportionate action. Appeals should be reviewed by a maintainer who did not make the original decision whenever possible. No response SLA is promised.
